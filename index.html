How to use the iTaoBuy spreadsheet

Select products and download your shortlist. Use one row per size and color combination, then fill in the missing details as you check the listing and contact your shopping agent.

  1. 1. Confirm the source listing and size

    Open the CAFANS product page and copy the original seller URL when available. Ask your agent whether it accepts that URL. Record the exact variation and compare the seller’s measurements with an item you already own.

  2. 2. Check warehouse QC photos

    If your agent provides photos after warehouse arrival, compare the size label, color, and measurements against your order. Flag any difference and confirm the return window before approving shipment.

  3. 3. Compare total costs

    Record the item price, domestic delivery, agent fees, and international shipping in the same currency. Compare quotes for the same destination and ask whether the carrier bills by actual or volumetric weight.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the iTaoBuy spreadsheet data come from?

The featured rows come from the CAFANS catalog. The linked CAFANS pages are the place to check current product details and seller links.

Can I paste a CAFANS product ID into my agent’s order form?

The numbers shown here identify CAFANS listings. Open the product page, locate the original seller link if available, and check which link format your agent accepts.

What does the CSV include?

It includes the names, categories, CAFANS IDs, reference prices, and CAFANS links you selected. It also has blank columns for the seller URL, size, color, QC notes, and shipping quote. You can open it in Excel or import it into Google Sheets.

Does the listed price include international shipping?

The reference price shown is for the product. Use a destination-specific quote from your agent to estimate shipping and check any additional fees separately.
